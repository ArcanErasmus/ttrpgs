# rules
- everyone is a Wild Card (design before name) or an Extra

## rolls
- "ace" explodes
- "raise" for every +4 over TN(which is 4)
- in opposed rolls, the acting char makes first roll and must meet at least basic success (TN, which is 4) or fails
    - then defender rolls and must meet or exceed (>=) first roll or acting char wins
    - winner uses opp's roll for determining raises
- crit failure happens on a roll of a 1 on *both* trait and wild die
    - cannot reroll crit failures by any means
    - since extras don't get wild die, if they roll a 1 and it's relevant to know if crit fail, roll additional d6 just to see if it is a 1 too
    - when you get extra dice on a roll (not counting aces), crit failure happens when >half of dice roll a 1
- unskilled rolls are d4(+wild die as usual)-2
- if you get a reroll, you reroll *all* dice and pick which set to keep
    - if you reroll into a crit failure, immediately stop and eat the crit fail
- when gm rolls for a group, roll one trait die plus wild die to treat as collective result of the group

## bennies (aka "hero points" in our game)
- PCs and the gm's wild cards start each session with 3 hero points, gm starts with a pool of 1 hero point per PC, gm's 
- use them or lose them, BUT if a session ends in the middle of a dramatic moment or combat, wait until resolving it before refreshing them
- gm can award them for clever actions, roleplaying, great feats of heroism, telling a story in character, or a great line or table moment
- when a player draws a joker during combat, *all* players get a hero point, if the players get both jokers everyone gets two
- any time the villains draw a joker, each enemy wild card gets a hero point and the gm pool does too
### using hero points
- reroll on any trait
- instantly recover from shaken, can interrupt another's action if desired
- spend as a free action to recover 5 PP
- prevent wounds or recover from shaken see soak rolls pp127
- draw a new action card, occurs after all cards dealt and edges/hindrances resolved, can do multiple
- reroll damage
- gm may allow play to spend a hero point to influence the story: find a clue if stuck, come up with a mundane needed object, push an npc to be more agreeable

## conviction
- special award granted withen a character experiences a great and meaningful victory or a painful and catastrophic misfortune
- receive a token, can spend it to add a d6 to all trait and damage totals until beginning of her next turn, this die can ace
- multiple tokens can't be spent to stack
- kept between sessions, no limit
- can spend to trigger or refresh limited-cooldown abilities
